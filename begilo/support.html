Begilo Support

Begilo plays a planned sequence of music at an event. You decide the order, the cut points and the moment each song starts; Begilo keeps the music going and never goes silent unless you ask it to.

Getting started

  1. Tap the import button and choose songs from Files or your Music library.
  2. Open a song to trim it: drag the two handles on the waveform to choose the part to play, and listen around each cut point.
  3. Arrange the songs in the order you want. Press and hold a song to drag it.
  4. At the event, use Event Mode for a simple screen with large controls. Hold the exit button for 2 seconds to leave, so it cannot be closed by accident.

Common questions

A song from my Music library cannot be imported.
Songs with DRM, including Apple Music subscription tracks, cannot be copied. Songs only in iCloud must be downloaded in the Music app first. Or use a DRM-free audio file from Files.
Does it work without internet?
Yes. Imported songs are copied into the app, and Begilo never needs a connection.
What happens on a phone call or if Bluetooth disconnects?
Begilo tries to resume the music by itself and tells you what happened.
The app was closed during my event.
Open it again. Begilo remembers the programme and where it was, and waits for you to press play.
Can I save a backup recording of the programme?
Yes. Use Export to render the programme to an M4A audio file, then save it to Files or share it.
Reset the app.
Delete Begilo from your iPhone. This removes the imported copies, programmes and exports. Your original music files are not affected.
