Begilo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Begilo is an iPhone app for playing a planned sequence of music at an event, such as a wedding. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it. In short: nothing leaves your iPhone.

What Begilo does not collect

Begilo does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. It makes no network connections. It does not upload your music, song titles, programmes or anything else anywhere.

What Begilo stores on your iPhone

  • Songs you import — when you choose a song from Files or from your Music library, Begilo makes a converted copy inside the app's own storage so it can play offline during your event. Your original file is not changed. Deleting the app removes the copies.
  • Your programme — the order of songs, cut points, fades and names, saved as small files in the app's storage.
  • Waveform previews — small data files used to draw each song.
  • Playback position — so the app can pick up where it was if it is closed during an event.
  • Backup exports — audio files you create with Export, kept in the app's storage until you save or share them.
  • Display preferences — for example which list view you last used, kept in the app's settings.

None of this is transmitted off your iPhone.

Access to your Music library

If you choose to import from your Music library, iOS asks for permission first. Begilo reads only the songs you pick, only to copy them into the app, and only on your device. Songs protected by DRM, such as Apple Music subscription tracks, cannot be imported. You can withdraw the permission at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Media & Apple Music.

Background audio

Begilo keeps playing when the screen is off or another app is in front, so your event music does not stop. It does not use the microphone and does not record audio.

Purchases

Begilo has no in-app purchases and collects no payment information.

Children

Begilo does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional feature that sends data — the change will be posted here and noted in the app's release notes before that version ships.
