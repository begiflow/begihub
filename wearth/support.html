Tiếng Việt

Wearth keeps your closet on your iPhone. Photograph your clothes, put them together into outfits, log what you wear each day, and see which pieces are really worth what you paid.

Getting started

  1. Add clothes with the camera, which takes several photos in a row, or pick photos you already have. Wearth removes the background on your iPhone.
  2. If the cut-out missed an edge, open the item and fix the edge with your finger.
  3. Add a price and a category so statistics can work them out.
  4. Build outfits from your items, or let Wearth shuffle your closet into a random outfit and swipe through each layer.
  5. Each day, log what you wore. Over time you see cost per wear, your best-value pieces and the ones you have forgotten.

Common questions

Does it work without internet?
Yes. Everything, including background removal, runs on your iPhone. Only buying Wearth Pro and importing an item from a shop link need a connection.
How do I back up my closet?
Open Settings in the app and create a backup. It is a ZIP file with all your photos and data; save it to Files, AirDrop it to a computer, or keep it anywhere you like. Your closet is also included in your iPhone's own backup if that is turned on.
How do I move to a new iPhone?
Restore your iPhone backup, or install Wearth on the new iPhone and restore the backup ZIP from Settings.
The background removal cut off part of an item.
Open the item and edit the edge: paint to erase or bring back parts of the photo. Photos taken against a plain, uncluttered background give the cleanest results.
What is the pet?
A companion that collects coins each time you rewear something you own, equal to the part of its price that wear has paid back. You can rename it, design your own, make it from a photo of your real pet, or turn it off in Settings.
Can you recover my data?
No. Wearth has no account and no server, so the only copies of your data are on your iPhone and in backups you made.
Delete all my data.
Delete Wearth from your iPhone. This removes all photos and data stored by the app.
