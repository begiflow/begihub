Wearth Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Tiếng Việt

Wearth is an iPhone app for keeping track of your clothes, your outfits and what you wear each day. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it. In short: nothing leaves your iPhone.

What Wearth does not collect

Wearth does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The app makes no network connections of its own. It does not upload your photos, your closet, your outfits or anything else anywhere.

What Wearth stores on your iPhone

  • Photos of your clothes — the photos you take or pick, the background-removed cut-outs and small thumbnails, saved in the app's own storage.
  • Your closet — item names, categories, colours, brands, tags, purchase prices, notes and status, in a database inside the app.
  • Outfits and wear log — the outfits you build and the days you record wearing them, used to work out statistics such as cost per wear.
  • Your pet — the companion character's name and look. If you choose to make it from a photo of your own pet, the cut-out of that photo is stored in the app.
  • Backups — ZIP files you create in Settings, and a few automatic backups, kept in the app's storage until you save or share them.
  • Settings — for example your currency and display choices.

None of this is transmitted off your iPhone.

On-device processing

Removing the background from a photo, finding your pet in a portrait photo and reading depth information all run on your iPhone using Apple's Vision framework. No photo is uploaded for processing.

Permissions

  • Camera — to photograph your clothes inside the app. Photos are saved on your iPhone only.
  • Photos — when you pick existing photos, iOS shows only the photos you choose; Wearth cannot see the rest of your library.
  • Motion — when a pet made from a portrait photo is shown, tilting your iPhone moves the picture slightly. The tilt is read on the device and is not recorded.

You can change these permissions at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security.

Shortcuts

Siri Shortcuts actions, such as logging today's outfit, read and write the same data on your iPhone. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Backups and your responsibility

Because your data exists only on your iPhone, the developer cannot recover it for you. Your closet is included in your iPhone's own backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if that is turned on, and you can export a full backup ZIP at any time from Settings and keep it wherever you like.

Purchases

Wearth Pro, an optional one-time purchase, is sold through the App Store. Payment is processed entirely by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer receives no payment details or personal information from your purchase; the app only asks the App Store whether Pro has been purchased on your Apple Account or shared with you through Family Sharing.

Third-party code

Wearth includes one open-source library, GRDB, which stores data in a local SQLite database and has no network component. It contains no other third-party SDKs.

Deleting your data

Deleting Wearth from your iPhone deletes all of its data. No copy remains anywhere else, except in backups you made yourself.

Children

Wearth does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional feature that sends data — the change will be posted here and noted in the app's release notes before that version ships.
