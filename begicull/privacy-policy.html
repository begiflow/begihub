BegiCull Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

BegiCull is a macOS app for reviewing video clips on your own Mac. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it. In short: nothing leaves your Mac.

What BegiCull does not collect

BegiCull does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. It does not upload your videos, file names, ratings or tags anywhere.

What BegiCull stores on your Mac

  • Your ratings, tags and clip names — in a file named BegiCull.json inside each folder you open, so they travel with your footage. You can delete this file at any time.
  • Recent Projects — the list of folders you opened, with macOS security-scoped bookmarks so the app can reopen them. Stored in the app's preferences on your Mac. Remove entries from the Home screen (right-click ▸ Remove from Recent Projects) at any time.
  • Thumbnails — small preview images, cached in the app's own container on your Mac.
  • Free Pro action count — how many of the free Export / Trash runs you have used.

None of this is transmitted off your Mac.

Purchases

BegiCull Pro is sold through the Mac App Store. Payment is processed entirely by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer receives no payment details or personal information from your purchase; the app only asks the App Store whether Pro has been purchased on your Apple Account.

Children

BegiCull does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional feature that sends data — the change will be posted here and noted in the app's release notes before that version ships.
