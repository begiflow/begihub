BegiCull Support

BegiCull is a keyboard-first video player for culling folders of clips on macOS.

Getting started

  1. Click Open Folder… (⌘O) or drop a folder on the window.
  2. Each clip plays as you reach it. Y accepts, N rejects, ← → move between clips.
  3. ⌘E copies accepted clips to a Selects folder inside the project.
  4. ⌘⇧⌫ moves rejected clips to the Trash (after asking).

Press ? in the app, or choose Help ▸ BegiCull Help, for every shortcut.

Common questions

Where are my ratings saved?
In BegiCull.json inside the folder you opened. Move the folder and its ratings move with it.
A rejected clip wasn't moved to the Trash.
Drives formatted ExFAT or FAT (most camera cards) and some network shares have no Trash. BegiCull then asks whether to delete those clips permanently instead; choose Keep Files to leave them where they are.
Which formats are supported?
.mov, .mp4 and .m4v — anything macOS itself can play in those containers.
I bought BegiCull Pro on another Mac.
Choose BegiCull ▸ Restore Purchases.
Reset the app.
Quit BegiCull, then delete BegiCull.json from a project folder to clear its ratings, or right-click a project on the Home screen ▸ Remove from Recent Projects.
